## Consolidated conclusion

The analysis covers **30/30 teams**: 9 dated official named lists, 1 aggregate-only case (Dallas), 7 secondary opening reports, 4 time-uncertain/post-cutoff reports and 9 partial/unresolved cases. These are research classifications, not admission verdicts. Washington's newly inspected official artwork supersedes its earlier aggregate-only assessment; Oklahoma City's inspected official season guide remains partial evidence because its historical edition timing is unverified.

Blocking gaps are (1) exact membership/contract classification, especially MIL/PHI/POR/UTA; (2) continuity and effective-time evidence; (3) complete financial ledgers for every team. Unknown money is not zero, and a waiver report does not establish extinguished obligations.
